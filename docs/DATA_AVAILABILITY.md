# Data availability and provenance

The epidemiological source is the Robert Koch Institute (RKI) StopptCOVID dataset, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.10888033`, licensed under CC BY 4.0. The source CSV is bundled for exact reproducibility and retains attribution and checksum metadata.

The semi-real communication corruption files are generated by this repository from the clean RKI trajectories. Missingness, delays, and outliers are artificial and must not be described as measured RKI reporting or network telemetry.

Population denominators correspond to 31 December 2020 and are documented in `data/metadata/population_by_state.csv` and `configs/data_config.json`.
